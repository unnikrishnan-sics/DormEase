ABSTRACT

The Integrated PG and Dormitory Management System with Mess and Analytics is a web-based application designed to simplify and automate the management of hostel and paying guest accommodations. The system handles essential operations such as user management, room allocation, student records, booking, payment processing, notifications, and complaint handling. It also includes a Mess and Food Management module that allows administrators to manage weekly menus, track subscriptions, and handle food-related complaints efficiently. Powered by modern technologies, the system integrates Google Gemini AI for smart complaint analysis and menu suggestions, Stripe for secure online transactions, and a dynamic QR-code system for contactless attendance tracking. The analytics dashboard provides insights into occupancy rates, revenue generation, and complaint trends, enabling better decision-making. The system improves operational efficiency, reduces manual errors, and enhances user experience. It is scalable, user-friendly, and suitable for modern residential facilities.

1.2 LITERATURE SURVEY

1.2.1 Hostel Management System

Existing hostel management systems focus on automating basic administrative tasks such as room allocation, student registration, and fee collection. These systems reduce manual workload and improve record management. However, most of them lack advanced features such as real-time analytics, integrated mess management, AI-driven insights, and complaint tracking systems. They are often limited in scalability and do not provide a user-friendly interface for both administrators and residents.

1.2.2 Online Room Booking Systems

Online booking systems allow users to check room availability and make reservations easily. These systems improve convenience and reduce manual booking errors. However, they mainly focus on booking functionality and do not integrate other essential modules like student management, payment tracking, or complaint handling. This lack of integration limits their effectiveness in managing complete dormitory operations.

1.2.3 Property Management Systems

Property management systems are widely used for managing rental properties, including tenant records and payments. While these systems offer robust financial tracking and reporting features, they are not specifically designed for hostel or PG environments. They often lack features such as shared room allocation, mess management, and student-specific services.

1.2.4 Mess Management Systems

Mess management systems handle food-related operations such as menu planning and meal tracking. These systems improve food service organization and reduce wastage. However, they usually operate independently and are not integrated with accommodation systems. This separation leads to inefficiencies in managing subscriptions and complaints related to food services.

1.2.5 Analytics in Management Systems

Modern management systems incorporate analytics to provide insights into performance and usage trends. These systems help administrators make informed decisions based on data. However, many traditional hostel management solutions lack advanced analytics features, limiting their ability to optimize operations and improve services effectively.

3.7 SAMPLING METHODS

3.7.1 Random Sampling

Random sampling is a method where data is selected randomly from the entire dataset without any specific pattern. In this system, random sampling can be used to analyze user activity, attendance records, or complaint data by selecting entries randomly.

3.7.2 Systematic Sampling

Systematic sampling involves selecting data at regular intervals from the dataset, such as every 5th or 10th record.

3.7.3 Stratified Sampling

Stratified sampling divides the data into different groups or categories (such as students, staff, and admin) and then selects samples from each group.

3.7.4 Cluster Sampling

Cluster sampling involves dividing the data into clusters, such as rooms or floors in a dormitory, and then selecting entire clusters for analysis.

3.7.5 Convenience Sampling

Convenience sampling involves selecting data that is easily available or accessible.
